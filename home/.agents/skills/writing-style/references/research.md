# Basis and limits

Profile assembled on 1 October 2026 from raw user requests and corrections in
local assistant sessions, conversational work-chat messages, and older issue
descriptions and comments. Private source text and locators are deliberately
kept outside this repository. Examples in this skill are invented.

The session search covered 1,779 saved files from June–October 2026 and found
73 unique candidate requests about wording, structure, brevity, or related
topics. Candidates included irrelevant material and automatic wrappers; they
were narrowed to the user's own relevant requests and corrections. The count
describes discovery, not 73 verified prose samples.

The final evidence anchors are 14 direct corrective requests, 11 short
conversational chat turns, and contextual prose or comments from five older
issues. Quoted draft wording inside corrections was not used as a voice sample.

External research inspected two sets of 60 work-chat posts and eight issues,
including comments from December 2024. An initial comparison of 130 external
items with 35,262 saved assistant messages found exact normalized eight-word
overlap in 44 items. Overlapping items were excluded conservatively, along with
release templates, agent-prefixed posts, quoted passages, and ambiguous long
drafts. The additional older issue prose was reviewed separately.

Public prose was also inspected. Translations, explicitly AI-written sections,
and posts with uncertain assistance history were excluded from the profile.
No blog post was used as a voice exemplar. Posting from the user's account,
approving a draft, or appearing before the saved session history does not prove
unaided authorship. Absence of textual overlap is a screening signal, not proof.
The profile therefore rests primarily on explicit user corrections, with
external prose used only where visible provenance supports human authorship.

## Strong signals

Repeated corrections asked for shorter, more concrete writing with less
bureaucratic phrasing; fewer unnecessary bullets; and concise but complete,
actionable descriptions. A correction that a draft had been compressed too far
sets the limit on brevity.

Assessment feedback specifically asked for responsibility followed by an
example, understandable context for an outsider, supported impact and
initiative, less incidental numerical detail, and fewer repeated cases.
Anglicisms and light technical slang were explicitly accepted in that context.

Conversational samples support direct proposals, brief greetings, practical
next steps, and ordinary expressions of uncertainty. Older issue comments
also distinguish measured observations from the author's judgment and allow
that judgment to change when new evidence appears.

## Confidence

Brevity, concreteness, useful context, audience adaptation, and prose-first
structure have strong direct support. Casual warmth and humor are secondary
signals; they are optional rather than mandatory stylistic devices. Evidence
for Russian workplace prose is richer than for unaided English long-form
writing. The English guidance is correspondingly restrained.

Prompt typos, one-off formatting choices, workflow authorization, and generated
status reports were not generalized into the author's voice. Future revisions
should favor new direct corrections over stylistic guesses or approved drafts.
